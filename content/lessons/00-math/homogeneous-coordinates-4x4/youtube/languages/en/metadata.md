## Description
```
A matrix can stretch, turn and shear a slime, but it can't slide it five steps to the right. In under five minutes, Inko the octopus adds one extra number, w: see how the 4x4 matrix keeps the move in its last column, why w = 1 is a point and w = 0 a direction, the normals trap, the T * R * S order, and the divide by w that makes far things small.

In this video:
- why no 3x3 matrix can move an object: M * 0 = 0 keeps the origin put
- homogeneous coordinates: (x, y, z) becomes (x, y, z, 1)
- the 4x4 layout: rotation and scale in the corner, the move t in the last column, 0 0 0 1 below
- why it works: a translation is a shear one dimension up
- w = 1 is a point and gets moved; w = 0 is a direction and doesn't
- transforming normals with the inverse transpose
- model matrices as T * R * S, and what R * T does instead
- the divide by w: x / w makes far things small
```
